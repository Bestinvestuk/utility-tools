BMI FAQ

What is a healthy BMI range?

According to WHO: Underweight (<18.5), Normal (18.5–24.9), Overweight (25–29.9), Obese (≥30).

Does BMI measure body fat accurately?

BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnostic one. It doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or fat distribution. Consult a healthcare provider for detailed assessments.
