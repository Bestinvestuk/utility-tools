Frequently Asked Questions

What is PMI and when do I need it?

Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) is required when your down payment is less than 20% of the home price. It protects the lender and typically costs 0.5%–1% of the loan annually.

How is the monthly payment calculated?

We use the standard amortization formula: M = P[r(1+r)^n]/[(1+r)^n-1], plus your annual taxes and insurance divided by 12.

Are taxes and insurance included?

Yes. Enter your estimated annual property tax and homeowner's insurance to get a realistic monthly payment.
